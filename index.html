What it does

One ledger for everyone you split with.

Someone covers dinner. Someone else gets the taxi. A week later nobody agrees on who owes what. BalanceLoop keeps that answer straight.

🍽️

Split it any way

Evenly, by custom amounts, or with several people paying toward the same bill. The maths is done for you and the history is kept.

👻

Even with non-users

Add someone who does not have the app and split with them right away. If they join later, their whole history comes across — once you both agree.

🤝

Settle honestly

Recording a payment asks the other person to confirm it, so a balance never changes just because one side said so.
